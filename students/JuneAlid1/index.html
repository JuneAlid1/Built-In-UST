What This Data Tells Us

Production figures help show the scale of Minnesota crop production. However, total production does not tell us how much is available for UST Harvest Supply to purchase.

Supplier prices, customer demand, harvest conditions, and product quality should also be considered before making a sourcing decision.

Data is stored locally in crop-comparison.csv. See decision-and-source.md for source information and limitations.
