# Harvest to Screen – OPMT 375

## Decision Question

Which product should UST Harvest Supply prioritize sourcing—natural wild rice, sweet corn, or sunflower seeds—based on Minnesota production availability, market value, and sourcing potential?

## Data Sources

1. Minnesota Department of Natural Resources, 2022 Natural Wild Rice Harvester Survey:
   https://files.dnr.state.mn.us/wildlife/wildrice/harvester-survey-2022.pdf

2. USDA National Agricultural Statistics Service, Minnesota State Agriculture Overview:
   https://www.nass.usda.gov/Quick_Stats/Ag_Overview/stateOverview.php?state=minnesota

## Data Notes

The wild rice figure represents estimated green, unprocessed natural wild rice harvested in Minnesota in 2022. Sweet corn and sunflower figures are Minnesota production estimates for 2025.

The figures come from different reporting years and use different units. They should not be treated as a direct comparison of available commercial supply. Public production data also does not show current supplier costs or manufacturer demand.

The data is stored locally in crop-comparison.csv.
